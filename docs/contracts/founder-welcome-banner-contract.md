# CAPPO Meridian Founder Welcome Banner Experience Contract

## Purpose

This contract defines the canonical creative, symbolic, layout, and acceptance requirements for the Founder-specific welcome banner experience in CAPPO Meridian.

It exists because prior visual iterations drifted from the intended direction and key requirements were lost between image revisions. This document is the single source of truth for future CAPPO design, mockup, implementation, and acceptance review for this feature. Future image iterations, UI mockups, and implementation work must be checked against this contract rather than against the most recent loose conversation or image revision.

---

## 1. Core Design Goal

The Founder Welcome Banner must feel:

- executive
- AMG-worthy
- refined
- mythic
- symbolic
- founder-specific
- intentional
- premium
- command-oriented

The banner should feel like the Founder's command chamber, with the guardian spirit beast present as a loyal sentinel awaiting the Founder's next directive.

**This is not a generic dashboard hero and not a fantasy poster page. It is a refined office-grade banner environment integrated into CAPPO Meridian.**

---

## 2. Shared Global Requirements

Apply these to both Founder 55 and Founder 88 unless explicitly overridden in their own section below.

### Overall Feel

- Must read as an office / executive command environment.
- Must be designed as a banner, not a standalone page composition.
- Must support integration into the dashboard above cards and controls.
- Must feel premium, dark, elevated, and restrained.

### Visual Tone

- Dark luxury palette.
- Gold accents and trim.
- Premium materiality: onyx, stone, metal, glass, subtle firelight.
- Intentional architectural feel.

### Founder Typography

Founder-only typography should be:

- kingly
- refined
- ceremonial
- supported by gold trim or gold-accent styling

This elevated typography treatment is for Founder-specific surfaces only.

### Banner Behavior

- Banner height must remain visually proportional to the dashboard.
- It must not dominate the page like a standalone cinematic poster.
- It should sit comfortably above cards and dashboard content.

### Meta Containers

Meta information should sit in dedicated background containers or chips, not as floating raw text.

Permitted meta examples:

- date
- quarter
- founder label
- guardian spirit beast title
- approved founder-specific context

### Creative-Only Asset Rule

For final banner-only creative assets:

- no dashboard cards
- no overlay text
- no AMG emblem
- no CAPPO UI chrome
- no labels
- no meta containers

The creative image should be clean artwork only. A separate integration mockup may layer text and UI over the banner.

## 3. Founder 55 Banner Contract

### Founder Identity

Founder 55's guardian lineage is: **Apho / Apis-Rhino**

Current working banner direction: **Golden Bull Sentinel**

### Mood

Founder 55's banner should be:

- brighter than Founder 88's
- more radiant
- more triumphant
- visibly regal
- more fire-lit and exalted

### Beast Placement

The guardian beast should sit **left of center**. This left-of-center placement is the positional benchmark for the banner system.

### Environment

Founder 55's environment should feel:

- luxurious
- executive
- command-oriented
- gold and fire present
- architecturally premium

It should still read as an executive command chamber, not merely a fantasy courtyard or outdoor monument.

### Banner Proportion

Founder 55's approved banner establishes the intended usable height and page balance for future Founder banners. Founder 88's banner should be comparable in size and dashboard relationship.

## 4. Founder 88 Banner Contract

### Founder Identity

Founder 88's guardian spirit beast is: **Dragon-Lion**

### Core Mood

Founder 88's banner should feel:

- cunning
- mysterious
- strategic
- interior
- stone-based
- Havenry-like
- less brightly illuminated than Founder 55's banner

### Architectural Direction

Founder 88's room should feel closer to:

- The Havenry
- dark stone interiors
- mystery
- intentional fire
- executive sanctuary
- private office / private chamber
- inward, contemplative, clever, Gemini-aligned atmosphere

It should not feel like an outdoor spectacle. It must feel more like an indoor chamber, executive office, or private sanctum.

### Materials

- More stone.
- More onyx.
- Less high reflectivity on floors.
- Floors should read as rich dark onyx, not mirror-polished glass.
- Reflections should be controlled and subtle.

### Beast Placement

Founder 88's Dragon-Lion must also sit **left of center**. This aligns visually with Founder 55's Apho / Apis-Rhino banner logic.

### Fire Element

Fire must be present. Founder 88's fire should feel:

- indoor
- ambient
- architectural
- controlled
- vessel-based or sconce-based

Examples: sconces, hearth-like fire accents, contained flames, vessel-based fire, candle/fire product elements. It should not read like an outdoor blaze scene.

## 5. Founder 88 Timepiece Contract

The timepiece is a critical symbolic and visual requirement and **must not be omitted**.

### Presence

Founder 88's space must include a massive feature timepiece.

### Placement / Construction

The timepiece should be an intentional architectural focal point. It must read as a real designed object in the room.

### Functional Clock Hands

The clock hands must:

- be centered correctly
- originate from the exact center hub
- have a visible central pivot
- function visually like real clock hands
- be gold
- be shaped like two swords

Do not allow off-axis or decorative-only hands.

### Inner Symbolism

The inner part of the timepiece must include:

- the Gemini zodiac constellation
- light points that feel like sconces or embedded luminous markers
- integration into the wall/timepiece architecture

### Hebrew Numbering

The timepiece should incorporate Hebrew numbering from 1 through 12.

### Outer Number Ring

The timepiece should also include the requested outer number system:

- outer numbers from 0 through 9
- 0 should occupy the top position, equivalent to 12 o'clock

If exact final mapping requires clarification, preserve this requirement and do not improvise a conflicting numbering system without Founder approval.

### Intentionality Note

Because Founder 88 is intentional about his space, the symbolic logic of the timepiece must be explainable in implementation notes or design rationale. The rationale should explain:

- why Hebrew numerals are present
- what the outer ring represents
- why Gemini is centered
- why the hands are shaped like swords

## 6. Founder 88 HVN Product / Object Contract

### Atmos Chamber Correction

A generic glass ball is **not acceptable** as an Atmos Chamber. An Atmos Chamber is equivalent to a candle in the basic world.

### Correct Product Direction

The requested object is closer to a proprietary HVN concept:

- a globe-shaped Atmos Chamber / Atlas Chamber concept
- an outer globe form
- the outer form conceptually melts down or opens
- the reveal exposes an inner secondary Atmos Chamber
- the inner Atmos Chamber should read as a regular candle-like Atmos Chamber

### Placement

This object should be visible in Founder 88's room. It should feel:

- proprietary
- intentional
- premium
- product-designed
- integrated into the executive sanctum

It must not look like a random generic orb or glass decoration.

## 7. Hebrew Israelite Flavor Requirement

Founder 88's banner should include Hebrew Israelite flavor in a disciplined, elegant, intentional way.

This should appear through:

- Hebrew numbering in the timepiece
- symbolic intentionality
- sacred-intellectual design cues
- covenant-like geometry if used sparingly
- dark stone and gold architectural gravity

Avoid:

- random letters
- decorative Hebrew without purpose
- clutter
- costume-like or poster-like religious styling
- symbols that cannot be explained

The tone should be: dignified, meaningful, esoteric, intentional, executive.

## 8. AMG Emblem Rule

### Incorrect Emblem Rejection

If an AMG emblem is shown in an integration mockup, it must use the correct AMG symbol only.

### Banner-Only Deliverable Rule

For final creative banner assets, remove AMG emblem entirely. The creative-only banner should contain:

- no AMG emblem
- no CAPPO wordmark
- no overlay text
- no dashboard cards
- no UI elements

## 9. Text / UI / Integration Rules

These apply only when the creative banner is integrated into the CAPPO dashboard.

### Welcome Greeting

The welcome message should use elevated Founder-only font treatment. The greeting must harmonize with the banner composition. Lower-third placement is acceptable when aligned with the visual composition.

### Meta Presentation

Meta should sit in background containers/chips.

Permitted meta content:

- date
- quarter
- Founder identity
- guardian spirit beast title
- approved Founder-specific context

### Card Relationship

The integrated dashboard version must allow the banner to sit above the cards in proportions similar to the approved Founder 55 layout reference.

## 11. Acceptance Criteria

### Shared Acceptance

A deliverable passes only if:

- it reads as a banner, not a full page
- it reads as an executive office / command environment
- Founder-only font treatment feels regal and refined
- meta sits in containers
- incorrect AMG emblem is not used
- creative-only version includes no text/cards/emblems/UI

### Founder 55 Acceptance

Founder 55 deliverable passes only if:

- beast is left of center
- banner feels brighter and more radiant than Founder 88's
- fire and gold feel premium
- proportion works with dashboard cards below
- it supports Apho / Apis-Rhino / Golden Bull Sentinel identity

### Founder 88 Acceptance

Founder 88 deliverable passes only if:

- room feels Havenry-like: stone, dark, mysterious, clever
- it is less illuminated than Founder 55's
- Dragon-Lion is left of center
- fire is present and indoor in feeling
- floor is onyx-like and not overly reflective
- massive timepiece is present
- clock hands are centered and functional
- Hebrew numbers are present
- outer number ring is present
- Gemini constellation is integrated at the center
- sword-shaped gold hands are present
- Atlas Chamber / globe-style Atmos Chamber concept is visible and correctly interpreted
- no generic glass ball substitution occurs

## 12. Non-Goals / Do Not Do

Do not:

- turn the banner into a full poster page
- omit the Founder 88 timepiece
- place clock hands off-center
- use incorrect AMG branding
- use a generic orb and call it an Atmos Chamber
- over-illuminate Founder 88's room
- make Founder 88's room feel exterior-first
- use overly reflective flooring
- clutter the design with unexplained symbols
- add text/cards/UI to banner-only assets
- modify PR #64
- begin GrantOps
- begin Contact Center work
- begin unrelated preview-lane work
